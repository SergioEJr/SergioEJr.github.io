## Complex numbers

$$
\mathbf{C} = \{ x + iy : x,y\in \mathbf{R} i^2 = 1\}
$$

$$
e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta
$$

## Dual numbers

$$
e^{\epsilon x} = 1 + \epsilon x
$$


## Split complex numbers

$$
e^{j \theta} = \cosh\theta + j\sinh\theta
$$
